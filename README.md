### Undergraduate thesis · In progress

My undergraduate thesis focuses on **primordial black holes (PBHs)**, their cosmological context, and their potential **gravitational-wave signatures**.

- **Supervisor:** Prof. Emmanouil Saridakis
- **Co-supervisor:** Prof. Theocharis Apostolatos
- **Topics:** primordial black holes · cosmology · general relativity · gravitational waves

### Research project · Supermassive black-hole populations
**Cosmic evolution of black holes in galactic centers**

Under the supervision of **Prof. Maria Petropoulou**, I worked on theoretical descriptions of how **supermassive black-hole (SMBH) populations** evolve over cosmic time. The project involved the physics of accretion-driven growth and active galactic nuclei (AGN), including:

- Black-hole mass functions and their evolution
- Eddington ratios and AGN duty cycles
- Continuity-equation approaches to black-hole population evolution

## Featured project

### [Primordial Black Hole Formation Lab](https://github.com/elisavetpavlidi/Primordial-Black-Hole-Formation)

Two interactive approaches to exploring primordial black-hole formation:

| Experience | What it does | Try it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Visual PBH Lab** | Illustrates gravitational collapse and dispersal through an accessible schematic model. | [Launch visual simulation](https://pbhformation.vercel.app/) |
| **Computational PBH Lab** | Experimentally evolves the spherically symmetric Einstein–Euler equations for a radiation fluid. | [Launch computational simulation](https://pbhformation.vercel.app/pbh_formation.html) |

**[Explore the source code and project documentation](https://github.com/elisavetpavlidi/Primordial-Black-Hole-Formation)**

> **Scientific scope:** The visual model is schematic. The computational model is an exploratory educational solver, **not** a research-validated numerical-relativity code.
