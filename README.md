# Elisavet Pavlidi

I'm a physics undergraduate at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, specializing in astrophysics. I'm interested in cosmology, black holes and gravitational waves.

For my thesis, I study gravitational wave signatures from primordial black holes with Prof. Emmanouil Saridakis and Prof. Theocharis Apostolatos. I have also worked with Prof. Maria Petropoulou on supermassive black hole populations.
